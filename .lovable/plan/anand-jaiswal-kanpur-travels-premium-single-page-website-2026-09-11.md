# Anand Jaiswal Kanpur Travels — Premium Single-Page Website

## Goal
Build a cinematic, conversion-focused one-page cab and taxi website for Anand Jaiswal Kanpur Travels Agency Pvt. Ltd., using the supplied eagle-and-flag logo and the provided business details.

## What will be built
- Sticky glass navigation with smooth section links and prominent call action.
- Full-screen photographic introduction with WhatsApp and phone booking actions, trust statistics, and subtle motion.
- About, services, premium fleet, benefits, booking process, reviews, call-to-action, contact, and footer sections.
- Floating WhatsApp action and a mobile sticky call action.
- Responsive layouts, accessible controls, reduced-motion support, fast image loading, and route-specific SEO metadata.

## Visual direction
- Luxury red, deep navy, and restrained gold on white/light gray.
- Poppins Bold headings and Inter body copy.
- Realistic premium travel photography, cinematic overlays, glass panels, controlled gradients, and soft shadows.
- Polished reveal and interaction animations without sacrificing speed or readability.
